    Questions about booking.

    How can I pay?

    In the app by UPI, card or net banking, or choose cash on delivery and pay when your clothes come back.

    Why not just message my laundry on WhatsApp?

    You can, and many people do. The app adds what a chat can't: a price for each item before you book, a fixed two-hour pickup window, every stage on one screen, and a photo of each item at pickup.

    Do I have to use Bubbles?

    No. You can also pick a service and a laundry yourself. Each laundry's page shows its rating, distance and prices before you add items.

    What if something goes wrong with my clothes?

    You report it from the order screen, and the pickup photos are attached for you. The order stays open until the laundry responds. Compensation amounts would be agreed with each laundry before launch.
